Clean Home privacy policy

App: Clean Home (com.cleanhome.tv), a launcher for Google TV. Last updated: 2026-10-26.

In short

Clean Home does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. It sends nothing about you to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your TV

Clean Home saves your choices in its own app storage on the TV. These are your layout, the apps you picked and their order, the wallpaper, and the Watch Next items you hid. A small counter decides when to show a usage hint. None of this leaves the TV. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Apps on your TV

Clean Home asks Android which apps on the TV have a launcher icon. It uses the list to show your apps and to let you pick them. The list is not stored outside the TV and is not sent anywhere. If you choose Uninstall in an app's menu, Clean Home opens Android's own uninstall screen. Android asks you to confirm. Clean Home never removes an app silently.

Watch Next (optional, off by default)

Watch Next is off until you turn it on. When it is on, and you have given the app the READ_TV_LISTINGS permission, Clean Home reads the Watch Next rows that other apps publish on your TV. These are titles and poster links. It shows them on the home screen. It does not send these titles anywhere. While the setting is off, nothing is read and nothing is fetched.

Poster pictures are loaded from the web address the other app published. This is the one place Clean Home uses the internet. The request goes directly from your TV to that app's image server, over HTTPS. That server is run by someone else, such as the streaming service. Like any web request, it can see your TV's IP address. Clean Home sends no account, no device ID and no list of your apps with it. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Posters are kept in a cache on the TV, up to 96 MB. Turning Watch Next off clears that cache.

Home button helper (optional)

Google TV does not let a launcher take the Home key by itself. So Clean Home offers an optional Android accessibility service, called Home button helper. You switch it on yourself in Android settings. Its only job is to open Clean Home when you press Home. It listens for the Home key and no other key. It does not read the screen, text, passwords or other apps. Turn it off in Android accessibility settings at any time.

What Clean Home does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service in the app. If you install from Google Play, Google itself may collect crash and performance data under its own terms. The developer can see only the totals Google shows in Play Console.
  • No sale or sharing of data.
  • Wallpapers are bundled in the app. They are not downloaded.

Children

Clean Home is not designed for children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
